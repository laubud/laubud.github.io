abstract: >-
 Compulsive Sexual Behavior Disorder (CSBD) is categorized as an impulse control disorder in the ICD-11. CSBD is characterized by repetitive and intense sexual impulses or urges, leading to uncontrollable sexual behavior despite adverse consequences. There is an ongoing debate about the etiology of CSBD. Theories that classify CSBD an addictive behavior suggest that neuropsychological alterations such as deficits in emotion regulation are present in CSBD. Prior research indicates that individuals with CSBD may experience difficulties in regulating emotions, but the extent of these difficulties is still debated.
